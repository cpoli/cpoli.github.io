# Glossary

**Hub**: this site, `cpoli.github.io`. It introduces Charles Poli and routes visitors to his work.

**Kit**: one of the scientific toolkits (mathematicskit, physicskit, chemistrykit, engineeringkit, earthsciencekit, financialkit, blockchainkit, tbkit). Each one has its own docs site under the hub's origin. Iguanas and Gators are *PayPal open source*, not kits.

**Package user**: a scientist, student or practitioner who finds a kit and might install it. One of the hub's two primary audiences.

**Hiring visitor**: a recruiter or hiring manager checking Charles's profile. The other primary audience.

**Reach**: how many visitors the hub sends on to a kit, a repo or a package page (outbound clicks). Stars and monthly downloads are the lagging check, not the measure.

**Off-site action**: a step that grows reach outside this repo, such as a post on another platform, a list submission or a profile link. Charles carries these out himself from a checklist.

**Project page**: a case study on the hub for Iguanas, Gators or tbkit. It covers the problem, Charles's role, the results and links to the docs and repo. It is aimed at hiring visitors and package users alike. The other kits have no project page; their cards point straight to the kit docs.

**Post**: a short article in the hub's Notes section, usually distilled from a kit's history of breakthroughs. It links out to the kit's gallery.
